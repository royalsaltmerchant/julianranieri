# Dictionary data

The local JSON shards in `data/` were generated on 2026-09-10 from:

- KANJIDIC2: http://ftp.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/kanjidic2.xml.gz
- JMdict English Next Generation: http://ftp.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMdict_e_NG.gz

Both datasets are maintained by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group and used under CC BY-SA 4.0:

https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

Before publishing an updated site, and at least monthly while the dictionary remains publicly hosted, replace the bundled snapshot with data generated from the latest upstream files and update the date above.
