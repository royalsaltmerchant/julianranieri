Classic ghost directory / Kimuraya Anko

Kimuraya Anko 木村屋安子

An early comic ghost pairing the stern, white-haired maid Anko with the small white cat Giko. Anko is commonly pictured with an anpan held in her mouth, matching the bread-shop wordplay in her name.

Technical context

A version of Kimuraya Anko was distributed from the creator's site by July 2001. The later Anko_re remake identifies itself as Phase 1.01 and was offered on the creator's dedicated Anko page in 2002.

Safety note
Creator-published Phase 1.01 package preserved by the Wayback Machine. It contains the complete ghost, shell, and balloon, including the legacy executable component aya.dll. Treat it as historical software rather than modern supported software.

Scope & method
This index covers ghosts first released-or independently documented as existing-from 2000 through 2007. Later updates and official redistributions remain part of those ghosts’ records. Sources accompany each entry, and undocumented details are marked unknown. This site does not mirror ghost files.
