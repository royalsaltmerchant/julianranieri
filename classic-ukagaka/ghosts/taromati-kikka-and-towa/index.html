Taromati — Kikka and Towa Taromati（橘花＆斗和）

A long-lived classic ghost pairing the silver-haired Kikka with Towa, a black cat who can also appear in human form. Taromati began in 2003 and received a major second-generation rewrite in 2007, accompanied by revised default artwork and an extensive collection of additional shells.

Technical context

Taromati is the formal ghost title; Kikka and Towa are its characters. The original Taromati was formally released on 2003-11-10. Taromati2, released on 2007-07-21, is a substantial rewrite rather than merely a replacement shell, but retains the same character pair. The distribution page explicitly states that its administrator is not the ghost creator and credits production to 8.

Scope & method
This index covers ghosts first released—or independently documented as existing—from 2000 through 2007. Later updates and official redistributions remain part of those ghosts’ records. Facts are attached to sources; missing or unverified information is marked unknown rather than inferred. This site does not mirror ghost files.
