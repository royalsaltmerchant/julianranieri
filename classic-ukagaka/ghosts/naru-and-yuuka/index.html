Classic ghost directory / Naru and Yuuka

Naru and Yuuka 奈留＆ゆうか

An early bilingual ghost featuring the gentle Naru and her tiny companion Yuuka. Naru was one of the period's so-called Four Angels and grew from an earlier PersonaWare persona into a recognizable early Ukagaka character.

Technical context

Naru began as a PersonaWare persona and was test-released as the ghost 偽奈留 on 2001-01-21. The archived creator site lists Naru m0.15a on 2001-12-06. Contemporary reporting records a development-continuation announcement in July 2002 and a new site that August. No later package is currently listed.

Scope & method
This index covers ghosts first released-or independently documented as existing-from 2000 through 2007. Later updates and official redistributions remain part of those ghosts’ records. Sources accompany each entry, and undocumented details are marked unknown. This site does not mirror ghost files.
