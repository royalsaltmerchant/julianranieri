Technical context

Mikage Sakura is the redesigned default ghost bundled with MATERIA-B, beginning with period 534. She is a separate record from the discontinued 2000–2001 Nise-Haruna/Nin'i-tan incarnation. The surviving official download is the complete MATERIA period 583 Windows package, not a standalone ghost archive.
