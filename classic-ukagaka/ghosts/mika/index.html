Mika 深果

A story-connected early ghost featuring Mika and her smaller companion Naoko. Mika began as an Asymmetry Epilogue shell, became a full ghost in May 2001, and continued under the kanji name 深果 through 2004.

Technical context

The lineage began as the shell みか。in Asymmetry Epilogue on 2001-04-16 and became a complete ghost on 2001-05-30. The renamed 深果 period 1 followed on 2001-09-10. The package identifier mika and the earlier hiragana title establish the reading Mika. The final period 9 release incorporates material from Asymmetry Epilogue.

Scope & method
This index covers ghosts first released-or independently documented as existing-from 2000 through 2007. Later updates and official redistributions remain part of those ghosts’ records. Sources accompany each entry, and undocumented details are marked unknown. This site does not mirror ghost files.
