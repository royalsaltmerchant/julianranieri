Futaba and Tadakichi-san 双葉＆ただきちさん

The second default character pair in the early Ukagaka lineage. Futaba and her creature companion Tadakichi-san were bundled with 何か during the period between the original Sakura incarnation and the later default Sakura redesign.

Technical context

Futaba became the bundled default character with 何か period “redo” zero on 2001-08-05. The contemporary redo terminology reference describes two selectable dialogue implementations: the Fake by 閑馬永空 and the Slayer by 橋本孔明. Period 284 replaced Futaba as the default on 2001-11-22. Surviving later files contain shells only, not the complete ghost.

Sources

  1. 「何か」システム用語集 “redo” 版 contemporary reference

    Contemporary reference identifies the characters as 双葉 and ただきち and credits the Fake to 閑馬永空 and the Slayer to 橋本孔明.

  2. 「伺か」システム用語集 “MATERIA” 版 contemporary reference

    Describes Futaba as the bundled character that replaced the earlier Sakura when 何か was formally released.

  3. 2002/05/23 - 何か最萌観戦記 そにょ41！ contemporary report

    Contemporary tournament coverage calls Futaba the second default and documents the pair's prominence in May 2002.

  4. 双葉＆ただきちさんを再配布するページ third party redistribution

    A 2018 third-party redistribution page citing permissive language from the ghost readme. Its linked ghost download is no longer available, but the page preserves the displayed screenshot and links to some separate shells.

Scope & method
This index covers ghosts first released-or independently documented as existing-from 2000 through 2007. Later updates and official redistributions remain part of those ghosts’ records. Sources accompany each entry, and undocumented details are marked unknown. This site does not mirror ghost files.
