Sakura and Unyuu さくら＆うにゅう

The original default character at the beginning of the lineage that became Ukagaka. Sakura-then known as Nise-Haruna and later Nin'i-tan-was created by 黒衣鯖人; Saxyun supplied the character artwork. Unyuu joined her in September 2000.

Technical context

The 2000-05-25 prototype preceded Unyuu, who was added on 2000-09-15. The character and software names changed repeatedly. This original Nise-Haruna/Nin'i-tan incarnation is distinct from the later Mikage Sakura redesign.

Scope & method
This index covers ghosts first released-or independently documented as existing-from 2000 through 2007. Later updates and official redistributions remain part of those ghosts’ records. Sources accompany each entry, and undocumented details are marked unknown. This site does not mirror ghost files.
