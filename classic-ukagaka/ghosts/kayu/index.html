Kayu 佳由

A story-connected ghost featuring the schoolgirl Kayu and her small doll companion Fuu. Kayu originated in LastLies prologue and was adapted into a desktop character at the end of 2003.

Technical context

Kayu was first published as a test release on 2003-12-30 and formally released on 2004-02-29. The creator's release history records eight subsequent periods through September 2005. The character originated in LastLies prologue.

Scope & method
This index covers ghosts first released-or independently documented as existing-from 2000 through 2007. Later updates and official redistributions remain part of those ghosts’ records. Sources accompany each entry, and undocumented details are marked unknown. This site does not mirror ghost files.
