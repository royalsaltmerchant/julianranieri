What is Ukagaka?

Ukagaka is a Japanese desktop mascot medium in which characters called ghosts appear on the computer desktop, display dialogue, and interact with their companions and users. A ghost’s visible character artwork is called a shell. The culture developed from software successively known as Nanika (何か), MATERIA, and Ukagaka, with SSP later becoming a widely used baseware.

Scope & method
This index covers ghosts first released—or independently documented as existing—from 2000 through 2007. Later updates and official redistributions remain part of those ghosts’ records. Sources accompany each entry, and undocumented details are marked unknown. This site does not mirror ghost files.
