About Me

I'm Julian Ranieri. I grew up in the Bay Area, and a lot of what I do comes from trying to understand how different systems fit together: music, software, games, communities, tools, the internet, nature, and whatever else I end up getting pulled into.


I think the common thread is that I like making things that have a clear point of view. That can mean a recording, a small desktop utility, a website, a tabletop tool, or even just a way of organizing information. The medium changes, but the question is usually the same: what is this thing trying to do, and what would make it feel right?


Music was probably the first version of that for me. I play drums and guitar, record and produce, and keep my music work at my music site. I also do sound design and audio tooling through SF Audio Guild.


Software became another way to work through those same questions. I have built a tape-style recorder, small utilities, encrypted chat infrastructure, and the Far Reach Co. virtual tabletop platform. I like clean architecture and performance, but mostly I care whether the thing actually serves the people using it.


I tend to be drawn toward creative tools, music projects, tabletop games, small communities, publishing experiments, and websites that still feel like they were made by a person.
